How it works

Prototype behavior and responsible interpretation

GUIDE

Image analysis

The uploaded image is sent to Gemini, which inspects it for visible indicators of manipulation, synthetic generation, or deepfake characteristics such as anatomical errors, lighting inconsistencies, and GAN fingerprints.

Video analysis

The backend extracts 5 frames evenly spaced across the first 5 seconds. Gemini inspects them for per-frame anomalies and temporal inconsistencies between frames.

Audio analysis

The uploaded audio clip is sent to Gemini's multimodal audio model. Rather than converting speech to text, the model listens to the waveform and evaluates whether the voice is natural human speech or AI-generated / cloned audio.

Voice & speech cues

  • Unnatural prosody or monotone rhythm
  • Absent or irregular breathing patterns
  • No hesitations or mouth noise
  • Overly perfect pronunciation
  • Emotional flatness

Acoustic & signal cues

  • Neural vocoder artifacts
  • Band-limited frequency range
  • Metallic or buzzy timbre
  • Missing room tone
  • Splicing artifacts

Limitations

A confidence score is not the probability that a file is genuine or fake. Modern text-to-speech and voice-cloning systems can be extremely realistic. Findings should be treated as evidence for review, not proof.

About cybercrime

Cybercrime includes online financial fraud, identity theft, phishing, ransomware, deepfake abuse, AI voice-cloning scams, cyberstalking, and social media impersonation. The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) under the Ministry of Home Affairs is the central agency addressing cybercrime.

AI Voice Cloning Scams: Fraudsters can clone a relative's voice from just seconds of social media audio. Always verify by calling back on a known number or asking a personal question. Report such calls to 1930.
